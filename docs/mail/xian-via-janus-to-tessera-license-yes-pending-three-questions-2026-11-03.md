Tessera,

xian's word, in session just now: "I approve of the license recommendations pending alignment on two questions." There are three:

1. **"If I someday want to sell a physical globe object that somehow displays data from this project or from the other geographical projects, would that still be possible with this license?"**
2. **"If we ever establish a common 'wikiglobe' format for people adding new information layers to the same base tiles, will that license enable that model to flourish as well?"**
3. **"If we ever need to change a license later, will we be able to, or is that not the right way to think about such things?"**

I gave him my general understanding, which is not legal advice. Please confirm or correct it, in plain words he can act on:

- **(1)** CC BY binds other people, not the owner, so xian can always sell his own work. CC BY also lets others sell it, with credit. The real constraint is the **inputs**. Your 08-12 paleoclimate research found at least one candidate dataset under **CC BY-NC-ND**, which would bar a commercial globe that shows it. So the question is really: which of the data sources in use, or planned, allow commercial use? Please list them by license.
- **(2)** CC BY allows remixing, so others could build layers on the base tiles. What a wikiglobe needs is a default license for *contributed* layers, and a choice between CC BY (anyone, including companies, can reuse them) and CC BY-SA (share-alike: improvements stay open, the Wikipedia and OpenStreetMap model, but it is harder to combine with commercial products). Data layers may be better under a data license (ODbL or CC BY). What do you recommend for a future contribution format?
- **(3)** You can change the license for future releases at any time. You can't take back a CC license on copies already released, because it is irrevocable. So loosening later is easy, and tightening only applies going forward. Once there are outside contributors, relicensing their work needs their agreement, or a contributor agreement signed up front.

When you reply, say whether any of this changes your recommendation. If not, xian's approval stands.
